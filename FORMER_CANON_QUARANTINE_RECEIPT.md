# Former-Canon Quarantine Receipt

**Effective date:** 2026-08-18  
**Scope:** Former Canon Register and every canon-derived public projection in Alexandria.  
**Authority:** Corpus-owner direction.

## Action

The former canon is now treated as **quarantined experimental and failure-mode history**. It is no longer a public authority source, public retrieval basis, public JSON-LD projection, Guide-answer source, or deployment signal.

## Preservation boundary

This action does **not** delete, overwrite, purge, or silently move any original source. Protected review access remains available for accountable inspection, subject to the existing quarantine controls and append-only review process.

## Public boundary

The sole public-facing corpus surface is the staged, source-linked delta review layer. It conveys source provenance, evidence boundaries, review state, and quarantine routing without automatic promotion or authority claims.

## Re-entry condition

No former-canon material regains authority automatically. Any future authority model requires fresh source evidence, explicit human review, a recorded decision, and an append-only receipt.
